An Android client for MCP

Your servers.
Your tools.
On your phone.

Universal MCP connects your Android device to Model Context Protocol servers. Save connections, browse available tools and review each invocation before you run it.

For Android 10 and later. Google Play release is in preparation.

Setup and support

Connect on your terms

Add an HTTPS MCP endpoint and choose the authentication its provider supports. A Microsoft Learn example connection helps you get started. Private servers may require your own network access.

Keep control of your activity

Profiles and execution history are stored on your device. Credentials are encrypted with Android Keystore protection. Optional argument and result retention is off by default.

Connecting to a server sends requests to that provider. Read its privacy policy before sharing sensitive information or running a tool.
